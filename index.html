About me

Engineer, researcher, developer.

I am an engineer from the UK with a PhD in Engineering Science from the University of Oxford and an MEng from the University of Sheffield.

My work is rooted in finite element methods, computational mechanics, and numerical simulation. I currently work as a software developer at Impetus AFEA, contributing to software for finite element, CFD, and SPH analysis.

I enjoy the craft of turning complex mechanics into tools that engineers can trust: accurate enough for demanding problems, clear enough to reason about, and practical enough to use.
